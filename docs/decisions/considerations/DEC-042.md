# DEC-042 — Consideration memo

Directive-first pilot completion and full-pilot scope confirmation. Follows the
eight-heading format of `docs/DIRECTIVE_PROTOCOL.md` § 1a. This is the pilot's
own follow-through (DEC-041), not a new direction; it is written for
completeness so the log is consistent and `G-M4` scans clean.

## 1. Direction restated

Complete the DEC-041 directive-first pilot on ephapse: land the two items
DEC-041 deferred (`needs:human` enforcement, bake-off sweep checks) and record
the two owner answers to the DEC-041 memo's §12 `NEEDS:`. Scope stays ephapse.

Verbatim quote (owner chat, 2026-10-01, no link available):

> "Can you continue the next steps to ratify this process as a full pilot on
> Ephapse? You are not held to the same standards as the new protocol but are
> meant apply the system and changes that are required."

## 2. Why now

DEC-041 is `Active` and its Phase 2 landed the protocol, the fields, the
consideration step, the grandfather cutoff (#88), and the `G-M4` scanner. Two
items were explicitly deferred in the ephapse notes, and the memo's §12 left two
owner decisions open. Waiting a week changes nothing technically, but it leaves
the pilot half-declared: the rules exist, and two of them are neither enforced
nor decided. The owner asked for the next steps, so they are taken now.

- **`portfolio-ops/METRIC_CONTRACT.md`** — reserves metric definitions to the

## 4. Options

1. **The directive as stated — complete the pilot.** Land the two deferred
   advisory checks, record both owner answers, keep warn-only. Cost: one run,
   low human attention. Easier: the pilot's rules are checked rather than
   trusted. Harder: nothing material; findings remain advisory.
2. **A narrower version — record the owner answers only, build no checks.**
   Cost: near zero. Easier: nothing to maintain. Harder: `needs:human` stays
   created-but-unenforced and bake-off slots stay unmatchable by the sweep —
   the exact "plausible artifact from an unchecked process" the repo's gate
   discipline exists to prevent.
3. **Do nothing.** Cost: zero now. Harder: the pilot stays declared-but-partial
   and the two `NEEDS:` lines stay open indefinitely.

**Recommendation: Option 1.** It is the smallest change that makes the pilot
internally consistent, and it adds no enforcement (still `--strict` opt-in).

## 5. Reversibility

Fully reversible. Both additions are advisory checks inside the existing `G-M4`
and two fixture files; removing them strands nothing and reverts to DEC-041's
state. No data, no branch, no credential is touched. The DEC-042 log entry is
append-only and would be superseded, not edited, if reversed.

## 6. Falsifier

The decision is wrong if the two new checks produce only false positives on the
live queue (forcing them off), or if recording the owner answers as a DEC
misstates a direction the owner did not give. Observable: a live-queue scan that
is non-clean for reasons unrelated to a real violation, or an owner correction
of the recorded answers.
